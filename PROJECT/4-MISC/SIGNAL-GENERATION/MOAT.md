Where HiQS is less copyable
The defensible part is not task prioritization by itself. It is the combination of:

Net-new signal detection: identifying teammate or delegated work that is absent from the operator’s own stream.

Redundancy suppression: down-ranking signals already visible elsewhere.

Dropped-ball prediction: learning which commitments quietly fail, not just which tasks are due.

Explainable local-first scoring: giving technical users inspectable reasons and tunable coefficients.

Outcome history: building a private corpus of commitments, completions, misses, handoffs, and recovery actions.

That is a much stronger claim because it moves the product from “AI ranks my todos” to “HiQS detects operational risk that my existing tools structurally miss.”
