## Lane A — GH-135 Pulse dashboard consistency refactor

- **Paths:** as listed above.
- **Contract:** the four phases in
  [GH-135-PULSE-DASHBOARD-CONSISTENCY.md](../1-INBOX/GH-135-PULSE-DASHBOARD-CONSISTENCY.md)
  — (1) add `format_timestamp()` to the existing `tz_utils.py` composing `format_local` /
  `format_relative`, and migrate all 13 `_ago()` call sites plus `_format_dt` /
  `_format_dt_short` onto it; (2) extract one shared row renderer with fixed anatomy
  (leading marker → title-first body + meta line → right-aligned timestamp) and zebra
  striping; (3) the seven per-module fixes; (4) one section-header casing system.
  Not re-derived here — the project doc's checklists are the contract.
- **Acceptance:** zero remaining call sites of the old formatters; no bare relative time
  in the DOM; every list through the shared row; no raw IDs / org prefixes / age strings
  in titles; before/after screenshots per module; `pytest tests/` green; `rebalance doctor`
  and `pdda.sh run` clean; page regenerated through `pulse_web.py` and verified live.
- **Out of scope:** any data-source, collector, route, or API-handler change; a second
  time module (GH-130's `tz_utils` is the single source of truth); hand-edits to
  `web/pulse.html` (a build artifact — the scheduler overwrites it); adopting the new row
  component on the Focus 5 / What's Next / Auth Log pages in this pass.
